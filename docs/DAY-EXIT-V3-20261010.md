# DAY exit v3 — floor plus late stall (the user's "Go", 2026-10-10; before the first real DAY trade of 2026-10-12)

The DAY trade's exit changes from the floor-and-trail rule of 2026-10-09 (v1: once 1.20x entry, sell at max(1.05x entry, 0.60x the
high)) to the floor plus a late stall, with no trail. The capped stall agreed on the evening of 2026-10-09 (v2, `DAY_REFINED.md` in
`~/Codex/2026-10-09-claude-codex-entries-exits`) was never deployed and is withdrawn: its evidence came from a 5-minute-close model that
cannot resolve a 300-second stall. Paper only; no configuration change; RULE_VERSION unchanged (the event carries the exit: `floor`,
`stall`, `trail: null`). The hash of this file is in `DAY-EXIT-V3-20261010.sha256`; any later change is an amendment, dated and
stated as such, made before the first-20-trade review is looked at.

## The rule (America/New_York; `rules.DAY_FLOOR`, `rules.DAY_STALL`, `broker.trail_due`)
- Entry unchanged: the 10:00 clock, the existing selector, the next trading day's expiry, CL calls and ES/GC/NQ puts, the 3% spread
  gate, the ES/NQ open-against veto.
- State: E = the entry fill; H = the highest valid bid since entry (starts at E); t_H = the time of the latest STRICTLY higher bid
  (an equal bid is a retest and never resets it). H and t_H are kept in the store across restarts (`trail_peak`, `trail_peak_at`).
- Before H >= 1.20E: no price exit. Once H >= 1.20E (armed for good): L = 1.05E; from 10:50 (the clock + 50 minutes, the event's
  `stall[0]`), while now − t_H >= 300 s, L = max(1.05E, 0.90H), at ANY H. Sell at the first valid bid <= L; the fill is the
  executable bid, never the level. The 300 s are elapsed time on valid quotes, not bars; 0.90H is never latched; a strictly higher
  bid removes the tightening until the high is again 300 s old. Hard exit 11:00 as before. No trail; no confirmation delay,
  spread postponement, partial sale, roll, pyramid, re-entry or extension.
- A DAY reservation made before this release (a plan with `floor` and `trail` but no `stall`) keeps the v1 semantics.
- Recorded on the first due quote (`trail_exit:<id>`): time, bid, high, the high's time, the cause (`FLOOR` or `LATE_STALL`) and
  the version `DAY-v3-fLS50`. The dashboard's exit-rule line shows the floor, the stall time and "no trail".

## Shadows and the first-20-trade rollback (judged from the recordings, not in the runtime)
`day_v3_shadows.py` in the hourly ledger replays, on the recorded per-second quotes of every DAY trade from 2026-10-12 and with the
same entry (the ask at the clock + 3 s) and the same fill convention, v1, v2 (capped stall: 1.20E <= H < 1.50E and the high >= 300 s
old → 0.90H, else 0.60H) and v3, and prints the paired Δ_i,j = r_v3 − r_j for j in {v1, v2}, the missed winners and the
deleted-trail failures. Revert the operating exit to v1 if, against EITHER shadow, within the first 20 actual eligible DAY entries:
ΣΔ <= −1.00 entry-premium units; or two distinct missed winners; or one missed winner costing >= 0.50. A missed winner = v3 exits
earlier, a later valid bid before 11:00 reaches 1.50E, the shadow finishes positive and beats v3 by >= 0.10. Deleted-trail failure
(also reverts): v1 makes a trail-only sale above 1.05E while v3 is still open, followed by one v3 shortfall >= 0.50, or by two trades
where v1 earns >= +0.10 and v3 finishes non-positive. A v2-comparison failure never re-promotes v2. At entry 20: coverage, changed
outcomes, missed winners, trail-only failures and ΣΔ by market. No retuning in between.

## Evidence (Claude + Codex, 2026-10-10; `DAY_PROFIT_RESULT.md`, `codex_profit_round2.txt` in the folder above)
1-minute SPY/QQQ bars (EODHD, 2025-02 → 2026-10), the option repriced every minute, next-day expiry, 0.25-delta, 10:00 → 11:00, 3%
toll, the open-against veto; n 696 [mean % / positives % / top-5% peaks kept % / peaks >= +20% converted %]:
  hold (no exit before 11:00)  +0.2 / 35.6 / 71.7 / 65.4      floor only (no trail)  −1.5 / 39.9 / 49.0 / 73.9
  v1 (floor + 0.60 trail)      −2.5 / 40.4 / 42.1 / 74.8      v2 (capped stall)      −3.6 / 43.1 / 35.2 / 80.2
  v3 (floor + late stall 10:50) −0.9 / 40.4 / 50.8 / 74.8
Paired, date-block bootstrap (Codex, 10,000 draws): v3 − v1 +1.61 pts [−0.01, +3.41], top-5% +8.7 [−1.9, +17.7]; v3 − v2 +2.74
[+0.76, +4.97], top-5% +15.6 [+3.2, +26.1]; v2 − v1 −1.13 pts, top-5% −6.9 [−14.9, −0.9]. The same paths thinned to 5-minute samples
at offsets 0..4 never reproduce v2's 5-minute gain (v2 − v1 −0.70 … +0.07 pts) and keep v3 − v1 positive (+0.64 … +1.88). The fact
behind the timing: with under 15 minutes left a 5–10-minute-old high is exceeded 23% of the time and a 15-minute-old one 10%; with
45 minutes left even a 10-minute-old high is exceeded 63–72%. All four markets at 1 min (n 1,553): v3 +0.9 / 44.0 / 50.6 / 76.4 vs
v1 −0.8 / 43.7 / 41.5 / 75.8. USO (the call side) is the one market where the trail still helped (v1 +2.8, v3 +2.0); it is reported
by market at entry 20, not special-cased.
Not established: superiority over v1 (the mean interval touches zero and the gain is second-half-driven); fill quality on floor sales
(on the 73 real same-day paths a 2–5 s fill delay flips the sign, driven by one GC quote); the real paths themselves (only 2 of 73
pass the 3% gate). What is established: v2's evidence was a sampling artefact, and v3 is never worse than v1 on positives,
conversion or the tail in either half of the 1-minute model.

## What changed in the code
`rules.py`: `DAY_STALL = (50, 300, 0.90)`, `day_stall()`, the DAY event carries `stall` and `trail: null`. `broker.trail_due`: the
`stall` branch (floor, then the late stall at any level; the high's time in `trail_peak_at`; cause and version in `trail_exit`).
`runtime.py` / `dashboard.js`: the position view and its exit-rule line carry `stall`. Tests: `test_day_exit_v3_floor_then_late_stall_
and_no_trail` (floor before the stall time, no trail, the late stall, retest and reset of the high's age), the invariant tests of the
DAY event; the pre-v3 DAY plan test is kept for the restart case.
