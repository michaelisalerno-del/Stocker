How it works

At 09:35 New York the 09:30–09:35 candle of each index CFD is compared with EMA12 of its 5-minute closes, overnight included: above → long, below → short. Each paper trade is sized so its stop (100 bp Nasdaq, 50 bp S&P) costs $200 and is held to the 16:00 close. CFD prices are always live.

How it works

The Nasdaq first-candle signal is also recorded as the same-day QQQ call (long) or put (short) with delta nearest 0.40, bought at the ask at 09:35 and sold at the bid at 13:00. Connors: at 15:50, QQQ above its 200-day average with RSI2(today) + RSI2(yesterday) below 35 → the next expiry's call with delta nearest 0.50, sold at the bid at 15:55 on its expiry day. While SaxoTraderGO holds Saxo's live slot, QQQ prices reach the server 15 minutes late: each QQQ step is then read 15 minutes after its time and recorded as of that time.

How it works

On a long first-candle day, each 10:00–15:00 close where QQQ's EMA10 crosses up through its session VWAP buys the Nasdaq CFD at the live ask; it is sold at the first close back below VWAP, or at 16:00. One at a time, no stop. A crossing not acted on within 60 seconds is recorded as a missed entry.

How it works

At 15:50 New York, on QQQ's daily closes with its live price as today's: Connors buys the Nasdaq CFD after a dip in an uptrend and sells at the first 15:50 with RSI2 above 65; Turnaround Tuesday buys after a Monday down 1% or more and sells at the next session's 15:50; the fear rule buys after three down closes with QQQ's 30-day implied volatility at 30% or more and sells at the fifth session's 15:50. Entries and the Connors exit check are missed while QQQ is late. No stop; results exclude financing.

How it works

Each evening the 1,500 most-traded US stocks are scanned on Saxo's daily bars for the worked example of the ConnorsRSI pullback: a 4% sell-off closing in the bottom quarter of its range, ADX(10) above 30, ConnorsRSI below 10. Each setup rests a buy limit 8% below its close for the next session, filled from that session's low; a filled stock is sold at the first close with ConnorsRSI above 70. Tier A is the top 500 by dollar volume, tier B ranks 501–1,500. During the session Saxo's price is read once a minute for the orders resting today and the open positions; fills and exits are still settled by the evening scan.
