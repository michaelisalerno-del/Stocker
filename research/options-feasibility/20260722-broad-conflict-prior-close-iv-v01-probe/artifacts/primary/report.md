# EODHD contract-history feasibility probe

The bounded probe completed for 3 frozen symbols and 9 stock-dates. Exact-date option observations were recovered for 9 targets and 8 passed the frozen primary-pair rules. It retrieved 3,624 provider records (1,029,673 bytes) through 27 recorded HTTP attempts with complete next-link pagination.

This is retrieval feasibility only. The completed V0 decision remains unchanged; no movement model, intraday option fill, option P&L, executable return, strategy result, or trading-utility claim was produced.
